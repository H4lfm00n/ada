## Who owns the equipment in the AI Studio

The AI Studio is inside the Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace (LIB 260). The computers,
3D printers, VR headsets, and checkout kits there are shared MakerSpace resources for
SFSU students, faculty, and staff. They are not AI Lab equipment, and the MakerSpace's own
rules apply.

## Shared equipment in the MakerSpace

The Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace has high-end computers with Adobe Creative Suite,
Final Cut Pro, and Pro Tools, plus scanners, printing, 3D printers, and VR headsets.

Its checkout list includes electronics prototyping kits: Arduino Mega 2560 and Uno kits
with sensors and motors, Raspberry Pi kits, micro:bit, and LittleBits. It also lends
cameras, microphones, recorders, lights, and tripods.

Using or borrowing any of these requires a valid SFSU OneCard and follows MakerSpace
rules.

Sources: Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace, at.sfsu.edu/digital-media-studio-and-makerspace (undated, checked 2026-10-29); Equipment Checkout, at.sfsu.edu/digital-media-studio-makerspace/equipment-checkout (undated, checked 2026-10-29).
