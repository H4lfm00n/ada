## SFSU Library

The J. Paul Leonard Library is at 1630 Holloway Avenue. It offers research help by chat,
email, or appointment. For general help, email ask@sfsu.libanswers.com or call
415-338-1552. Library hours vary by day and service, so check the live hours calendar at
sfsu.libcal.com.

Source: J. Paul Leonard Library, library.sfsu.edu (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Help choosing classes: Undergraduate Advising Center

The Undergraduate Advising Center helps with degree planning, choosing classes, exploring
majors, and policy questions. It is in ADM 203, at uacadvising@sfsu.edu and
415-338-2101. Its office and phone hours are Monday through Friday, 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM.
Appointments are offered in person and online; drop-in availability varies by advising
team.

Source: Undergraduate Advising Center, advising.sfsu.edu (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Career help: Career and Leadership Development

Career and Leadership Development offers resume and interview help, career events,
Handshake jobs and internships, and Hiration career tools. It is in ADM 254, at
career@sfsu.edu and 415-338-1764. Its hours are Monday through Friday, 8:30 AM to
4:30 PM, with drop-ins from 10 to 11 AM.

Source: Career and Leadership Development, career.sfsu.edu (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Free tutoring: Tutoring and Academic Support Center

The Tutoring and Academic Support Center (TASC) offers free peer tutoring in LIB 220 and
online. Contact tutoring@sfsu.edu or 415-405-5516. For Fall 2026, tutoring runs from
August 24 to December 16. In-person hours are Monday through Thursday, 9 AM to 5 PM, and
Friday, 10 AM to 2 PM. You can book a one-time appointment or request weekly support on
its website.

## Wi-Fi, password, and account help: ITS Service Desk

The ITS Service Desk handles password, wireless, and account problems. It is in ADM 125,
at service@sfsu.edu and 415-338-1420. Its hours are Monday through Friday, 8 AM to 5 PM,
with Zoom support from 9 AM to 4 PM.

Source: Get Help, Information Technology Services, its.sfsu.edu/support (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Disability access: Disability Programs and Resource Center

The Disability Programs and Resource Center (DPRC) helps with accommodations and
disability-related support. It is in Student Services Building room 110, at
dprc@sfsu.edu and 415-338-2472, with a video phone at 415-335-7210. Its hours are Monday
through Friday, 8:30 AM to 5 PM.

Source: DPRC Student Portal, access.sfsu.edu/student (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Counseling: Counseling and Psychological Services

Counseling and Psychological Services (CAPS) offers counseling, same-day support,
drop-ins, groups, and workshops. It is on the third floor of the Gator Student Health
Center, 730 Font Boulevard, at caps@sfsu.edu and 415-338-2208. Its hours are Monday
through Friday, 8 AM to 4:45 PM.

If someone is in crisis right now, they should call or text 988, or call 911 if they are
in immediate danger.

Source: Counseling and Psychological Services, caps.sfsu.edu (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Health care: Student Health Services

Student Health Services offers confidential basic health care, primary care, a pharmacy,
and lab services. It is on the second floor of the Gator Student Health Center, 730 Font
Boulevard, at StudentHealthServices@sfsu.edu and 415-338-1251. Its hours are Monday
through Thursday, 8:15 AM to 4:45 PM, and Friday, 9:15 AM to 4:45 PM. For a medical
emergency, call 911.

Source: Student Health Services, health.sfsu.edu (undated, checked 2026-10-29).
