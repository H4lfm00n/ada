## AI in the Computer Science bachelor's degree

SFSU's Computer Science department offers a B.S. in Computer Science, a Computer Science
minor, and a Computing Applications minor. The B.S. includes artificial intelligence as
one of its elective areas.

Source: Undergraduate Program, Department of Computer Science, cs.sfsu.edu/undergrads/undergraduate-program (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## AI master's degrees at SFSU

SFSU's Computer Science department offers an M.S. in Computer Science and an M.S. in Data
Science and Artificial Intelligence. According to the SFSU Bulletin, the M.S. in Data
Science and Artificial Intelligence requires at least 30 units and combines computer
science, statistics, machine learning, AI, and hands-on system building.

Sources: Graduate Program, cs.sfsu.edu/grads/graduate-program; M.S. in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence, bulletin.sfsu.edu (both undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Certificate in Generative AI for Computer Scientists

The SFSU Bulletin lists a 12-unit Certificate in Generative Artificial Intelligence for
Computer Scientists. According to the Bulletin, admission requires current undergraduate
status, a C or better in CSC 413 or an equivalent course, and a GPA of at least 3.0. Some
electives have extra prerequisites.

Source: Certificate in Generative Artificial Intelligence for Computer Scientists, bulletin.sfsu.edu (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Graduate Certificate in Ethical Artificial Intelligence

The SFSU Bulletin lists a 10-unit Graduate Certificate in Ethical Artificial Intelligence.
It is interdisciplinary, covering AI technology, business ethics and compliance, and
philosophical ethics, with a research and reflection component.

Source: Graduate Certificate in Ethical Artificial Intelligence, bulletin.sfsu.edu (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Minor in AI for Business Applications

The SFSU Bulletin lists a Minor in Artificial Intelligence for Business Applications,
offered through the Lam Family College of Business, with a minimum of 12 units.

Source: Minor in Artificial Intelligence for Business Applications, bulletin.sfsu.edu (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Who to ask about AI programs

For requirements, eligibility, and planning, check the program's page in the SFSU Bulletin
at bulletin.sfsu.edu, or talk to the Undergraduate Advising Center in ADM 203
(415-338-2101).

Sources: SFSU Bulletin, bulletin.sfsu.edu; Undergraduate Advising Center, advising.sfsu.edu (both undated, checked 2026-10-29).
