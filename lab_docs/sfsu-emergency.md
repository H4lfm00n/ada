## What to do in an emergency on campus

In an emergency, call 911. SFSU's University Police publishes these instructions for a
campus emergency: dial 911 from a campus phone, or 415-338-2222 from a cell phone.

Source: Crime Prevention, University Police Department, upd.sfsu.edu/crime-prevention (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Campus emergency phones

Marked emergency phones on campus connect directly to University Police dispatch. They
are in places including the Library and every level of the main parking garage. Campus
maps mark them with an "E".

Source: Campus Emergency Phones, upd.sfsu.edu/campus-emergency-phones (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## University Police non-emergency number

For non-emergencies and general information, University Police is at 415-338-7200,
staffed 24 hours a day. The email upd@sfsu.edu is not monitored around the clock and is
not for urgent help.

Source: Contact Us, University Police Department, upd.sfsu.edu/contact-us (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## SFSU Alert emergency notifications

SFSU Alert sends emergency information and instructions. Students can add or update a
"Mobile Text" phone number in Student Center, and employees use HR Self-Service.
According to SFSU's Office of Emergency Services, emergency email notifications are
mandatory for everyone with an SFSU email address.

Source: Emergency Notification System, oes.sfsu.edu/get-prepared/emergency-notification-system (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Mental health crisis help

If someone is thinking about suicide or self-harm, or is in deep distress, they can call
or text 988, the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, any time. If they are in immediate danger,
call 911. For ongoing support, SFSU students can contact Counseling and Psychological
Services (CAPS) at 415-338-2208, Monday through Friday, 8 AM to 4:45 PM.

Sources: 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline (national); Counseling and Psychological Services, caps.sfsu.edu (undated, checked 2026-10-29).
