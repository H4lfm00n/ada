## Student AI Commons Leadership Committee

The SFSU Student AI Commons has a student Leadership Committee that helps decide the
group's direction. The Leadership Committee meets monthly. Its kickoff on September 23,
2026 was open to all students.

Source: Leadership Committee kickoff, ai.sfsu.edu/event/sfsu-ai-student-commons-leadership-committee-kick (event 2026-09-23, checked 2026-10-29); President's Messages, planning.sfsu.edu/presidents-messages (source date 2026-10-01, checked 2026-10-29).

## AI Commons Coordinator role

A public post has described an "AI Commons Coordinator" role at the SFSU Student AI
Commons. No official list of Student AI Commons officers has been published.

Source: public social-media post (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## How to contact the Student AI Commons

The public email for SFSU's AI site and the Student AI Commons is ai@sfsu.edu. It is
listed on the Student AI Commons Leadership Committee event pages.

Source: Leadership Committee October meeting, ai.sfsu.edu/event/sfsu-ai-student-commons-leadership-committee-october-meeting (event 2026-10-27, checked 2026-10-29).

## Contact for Student AI Commons events and partnerships

To schedule an event or set up a partnership with the SFSU Student AI Commons, email
Academic Technology at at@sfsu.edu.

Source: SFSU Student AI Commons | Events, at.sfsu.edu/event/student-ai-commons (undated, checked 2026-10-29).
