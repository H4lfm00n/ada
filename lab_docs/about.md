## What the SFSU Student AI Commons is

The SFSU Student AI Commons is a student-centered community at San Francisco State
University. The Student AI Commons brings students, student organizations, and campus
partners together through AI workshops, projects, events, and peer collaboration.

Source: Academic Technology Updates: Fall 2026, at.sfsu.edu/news/academic-technology-updates-fall-2026 (source date 2026-08-24, checked 2026-10-29).

## Goals of the Student AI Commons

The Student AI Commons' stated goals include AI literacy, experimentation, and connecting AI
to students' academic, creative, and professional interests.

Source: Academic Technology Updates: Fall 2026, at.sfsu.edu/news/academic-technology-updates-fall-2026 (source date 2026-08-24, checked 2026-10-29).

## The AI Lab and the Student AI Commons

The SFSU Student AI Commons brings together SFSU students, student organizations, and
campus partners to explore artificial intelligence through projects, workshops, and
collaboration. The AI Lab supports the Student AI Commons through hands-on project work and
access to AI and robotics resources in the Digital Media Studio (DMS) in LIB 260.

Source: AI Lab PM (Atharva), confirmed 2026-11-10.

## What the AI Studio is

The AI Studio is a hub that serves the SFSU Student AI Commons. The AI Studio is inside the
Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace, in room LIB 260, on the second floor of the J. Paul
Leonard Library. SFSU's Academic Technology office announced the AI Studio.

Source: Academic Technology Updates: Fall 2026, at.sfsu.edu/news/academic-technology-updates-fall-2026 (source date 2026-08-24, checked 2026-10-29); AI Lab PM, confirmed 2026-11-10 (library building).

## AI Studio grand opening

The AI Studio held a free grand opening on September 29, 2026, from noon to 2 PM, in
LIB 260. That event was the AI Studio's opening. It is not the founding date of the
Student AI Commons.

Source: AI Studio Grand Opening, ai.sfsu.edu/event/ai-studio-grand-opening (event 2026-09-29, checked 2026-10-29).

## AI Studio hours

The AI Studio is inside the Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace. The MakerSpace's semester
hours are Monday through Thursday, 10 AM to 7 PM, and Friday, 10 AM to 5 PM. No separate
AI Studio schedule has been published.

Source: Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace: November events, at.sfsu.edu/news/digital-media-studio-and-makerspace-november-events (source date 2026-10-28, checked 2026-10-29).

## What Student AI Commons events feel like

Student AI Commons events are informal and hands-on. An October 2025 Commons workshop
described an "AI Lounge" for chatting, experimenting, and learning together, and required
no experience.

Source: AI Commons: ChatGPT 101, library.sfsu.edu/event/ai-commons-chatgpt-101 (event 2025-10-14, historical, checked 2026-10-29).

## The CSU AI Commons is a different thing

The CSU AI Commons is a set of systemwide California State University AI resources,
including responsible-use guidance. The CSU AI Commons is separate from SFSU's Student AI
Commons and from the AI Studio in LIB 260.

Source: AI for Students, ai.sfsu.edu/ai-students (undated, checked 2026-10-29).
