## What the SFSU Student AI Commons is

The SFSU Student AI Commons is a student-centered community at SFSU for AI workshops,
events, projects, and peer collaboration. It brings together students, student
organizations, and campus partners. Its stated goals include AI literacy,
experimentation, and connecting AI to students' academic, creative, and professional
interests.

Source: Academic Technology Updates: Fall 2026, at.sfsu.edu/news/academic-technology-updates-fall-2026 (source date 2026-08-24, checked 2026-10-29).

## The CSU AI Commons is a different thing

The CSU AI Commons is a California State University systemwide set of AI resources,
including responsible-use guidance. It is separate from SFSU's Student AI Commons and the
AI Studio in LIB 260.

Source: AI for Students, ai.sfsu.edu/ai-students (undated, checked 2026-10-29).
