## AI Studio and MakerSpace hours

The Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace, which contains the AI Studio, is open Monday
through Thursday, 10 AM to 7 PM, and Friday, 10 AM to 5 PM, during the semester. No
separate AI Studio hours have been published.

Source: Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace: November events, at.sfsu.edu/news/digital-media-studio-and-makerspace-november-events (source date 2026-10-28, checked 2026-10-29).

## AI Lab compute rules

Remote access to AI Lab computers is not available. Overnight computing jobs are allowed
only if they were started in person at the lab. GPU booking goes through the AI Lab team
(ai@sfsu.edu).

Source: AI Lab PM (Atharva), confirmed 2026-11-10.

## Borrowing cameras, microphones, and other A/V equipment

MakerSpace A/V equipment checkout is first come, first served, with priority for students.
Borrowers need a valid SFSU OneCard and the required training. Loans last up to three
business days. Checkouts and returns stop one hour before the MakerSpace closes. Late
returns cost $50 per day, and loss or damage can cost up to $2,000 in replacement fees.

Source: Equipment Checkout | Digital Media Studio & MakerSpace, at.sfsu.edu/digital-media-studio-makerspace/equipment-checkout (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Borrowing Arduino, Raspberry Pi, and robotics kits

MakerSpace robotics and mechatronics kits need a valid SFSU OneCard. Loans last four hours
and can be extended with approval. Kits must be returned in their original condition.

Source: Equipment Checkout | Digital Media Studio & MakerSpace, at.sfsu.edu/digital-media-studio-makerspace/equipment-checkout (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## 3D printing rules

MakerSpace staff approve and slice every 3D print. Models must be lawful and
noncommercial, and weapons, violent, or inappropriate models are not allowed. Submit an STL
file. Prints must finish within seven hours, and overnight printing is not allowed. PLA
costs $0.084 per gram, with a $1 minimum, paid in advance.

Source: Services | Digital Media Studio & MakerSpace, at.sfsu.edu/digital-media-studio-makerspace/services (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Laser cutting rules

Only MakerSpace staff operate the laser cutter. Laser cutting needs an appointment and
MakerSpace-provided or approved materials.

Source: Services | Digital Media Studio & MakerSpace, at.sfsu.edu/digital-media-studio-makerspace/services (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## VR session rules

MakerSpace VR sessions need a valid SFSU ID, check-in, a signed waiver, a spotter, and at
least two people. Sessions last 15 minutes, and availability depends on staffing and
scheduling.

Source: Services | Digital Media Studio & MakerSpace, at.sfsu.edu/digital-media-studio-makerspace/services (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Audio room and sewing room rules

The MakerSpace audio rooms are for audio work only, with no food or drink. Audio rooms are
generally limited to two hours per day, with extensions when available. Rooms and
equipment must not be left unattended. The sewing room is walk-in, with a two-hour limit
when busy. The Juki sewing machine is only for current students who already know how to
use it.

Source: Rooms & Spaces | Digital Media Studio & MakerSpace, at.sfsu.edu/digital-media-studio-makerspace/rooms-spaces (undated, checked 2026-10-29).
