## Student AI clubs that meet weekly

Three student clubs hold weekly meetings in coordination with the Student AI Commons:
AI Agents Club, AI Organization, and Graduate Business AI Club. Their schedules are listed
on their GatorXperience pages, which are linked from the Commons programming page at
at.sfsu.edu/event/student-ai-commons.

## Past event: the first SFSU AI hackathon

SFSU's first AI hackathon, the SF Hacks x GDG AI Hackathon, took place in early October
2026. It was organized by SF Hacks and the campus Google Developer Group, sponsored by
the Student AI Commons, and designed for beginners as well as experienced builders.

Sources: SFSU hosts inaugural AI hackathon, news.sfsu.edu/news/sfsu-hosts-inaugural-ai-hackathon (source date 2026-10-08, checked 2026-10-29); Meet the winning teams, ai.sfsu.edu/news/meet-winning-teams-2026-sf-hacks-x-gdg-ai-hackathon (source date 2026-10-05, checked 2026-10-29).
