## What DMS is

DMS stands for the Digital Media Studio, part of SFSU's Digital Media Studio and
MakerSpace. It serves SFSU students, faculty, and staff working on digital media projects.
The AI Studio, which serves the SFSU Student AI Commons, is a space inside the Digital
Media Studio and MakerSpace.

Source: Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace, at.sfsu.edu/digital-media-studio-and-makerspace (undated, checked 2026-10-29); AI Studio Grand Opening, ai.sfsu.edu/event/ai-studio-grand-opening (event 2026-09-29, checked 2026-10-29).

## Where the Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace is

The Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace is in room LIB 260, on the second floor. Desk phone:
415-338-1490. Email: at@sfsu.edu. Instagram: @sfsu.dms.makerspace.

Source: Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace, at.sfsu.edu/digital-media-studio-and-makerspace (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace hours

The Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace's semester hours are Monday through Thursday,
10 AM to 7 PM, and Friday, 10 AM to 5 PM. It offers drop-in exploration during those hours.

Source: Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace: November events, at.sfsu.edu/news/digital-media-studio-and-makerspace-november-events (source date 2026-10-28, checked 2026-10-29).

## Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace equipment

The Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace has high-end computers, Adobe Creative Suite,
Final Cut Pro, Pro Tools, scanners, printing, 3D printers (UM3 and UMS5), a laser cutter
(Epilog Fusion Edge), a Cricut Explore 4, and VR headsets. Checkout items include photo,
video, and 360 cameras (such as the Canon EOS RP and GoPro HERO 11), RØDE microphones,
recorders, lights, tripods, and electronics kits (Arduino Mega 2560 and Uno, micro,
LittleBits, and Raspberry Pi).

Source: Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace, at.sfsu.edu/digital-media-studio-and-makerspace; Equipment Checkout, at.sfsu.edu/digital-media-studio-makerspace/equipment-checkout; Services, at.sfsu.edu/digital-media-studio-makerspace/services (all undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace rooms

The Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace has rooms for audio production and mixing,
fabrication, green screen and multi-use, podcasting, sewing, and VR development. The
podcast setup has a RØDECaster Pro II, four PodMic microphones, headphones, and an iMac.

Source: Rooms & Spaces | Digital Media Studio & MakerSpace, at.sfsu.edu/digital-media-studio-makerspace/rooms-spaces (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Edge Lab (College of Business)

The MakerSpace rooms page also lists the Edge Lab (Lam Family College of Business) at
BUS 205C. The Edge Lab is a separate location, not inside LIB 260.

Source: Rooms & Spaces | Digital Media Studio & MakerSpace, at.sfsu.edu/digital-media-studio-makerspace/rooms-spaces (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Cricut cutting machine

The MakerSpace's Cricut Explore 4 is for decals, labels, stickers, and cardstock projects.
Users need basic operating knowledge. Limited materials are provided for small projects.

Source: Services | Digital Media Studio & MakerSpace, at.sfsu.edu/digital-media-studio-makerspace/services (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Can I just stop by the MakerSpace?

Yes. The Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace offers drop-in exploration during its posted
semester hours. Individual equipment and rooms have extra rules.

Source: Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace: November events, at.sfsu.edu/news/digital-media-studio-and-makerspace-november-events (source date 2026-10-28, checked 2026-10-29).

## Can I use the laser cutter myself?

No. Only MakerSpace staff operate the laser cutter, and it needs an appointment and
approved materials.

Source: Services | Digital Media Studio & MakerSpace, at.sfsu.edu/digital-media-studio-makerspace/services (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Can I record a podcast at the MakerSpace?

Yes. The MakerSpace has a four-person podcast setup and an audio-only room, generally
limited to two hours a day, with extensions when no one else has reserved it.

Source: Rooms & Spaces | Digital Media Studio & MakerSpace, at.sfsu.edu/digital-media-studio-makerspace/rooms-spaces (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Can a member of the public borrow MakerSpace equipment?

The MakerSpace's published checkout rules require a valid SFSU OneCard. Public borrowing
has not been published as an option.

Source: Equipment Checkout | Digital Media Studio & MakerSpace, at.sfsu.edu/digital-media-studio-makerspace/equipment-checkout (undated, checked 2026-10-29).
