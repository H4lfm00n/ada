## About SFSU

According to SFSU, San Francisco State University is a public urban university in San
Francisco and part of the California State University (CSU) system. SFSU's mission
emphasizes academic excellence, creativity, a diverse community, social justice, and
positive change.

Source: About San Francisco State University, sfsu.edu/about (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## SFSU address and main phone number

SFSU's main campus address is 1600 Holloway Avenue, San Francisco, CA 94132. SFSU's
university information phone number is 415-338-1111.

Source: About San Francisco State University, sfsu.edu/about (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## SFSU campus map

SFSU's campus-map site, maps.sfsu.edu, describes a 144-acre main campus in southwest San

Student Services at F4, and the Gator Health Center at G3.

Source: Welcome | Campus Maps, maps.sfsu.edu (page undated; map image labeled May 2025; checked 2026-10-29).

## Computer Science degrees at SFSU

SFSU's Department of Computer Science offers a B.S. in Computer Science, a Computer Science
minor, and a Computing Applications minor. The B.S. in Computer Science includes AI among
its elective areas. At the graduate level, the department links an M.S. in Computer
Science and an M.S. in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence.

Source: Undergraduate Program, cs.sfsu.edu/undergrads/undergraduate-program; Graduate Program, cs.sfsu.edu/grads/graduate-program (both undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## M.S. in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence

According to the SFSU Bulletin, the M.S. in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence
requires at least 30 units. It combines computer science, statistics, machine learning,
AI, and practical system building.

Source: SFSU Bulletin, bulletin.sfsu.edu/colleges/science-engineering/computer-science/ms-data-science-artificial-intelligence (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## AI certificates and minors at SFSU

The SFSU Bulletin lists three more AI programs. The Certificate in Generative Artificial
Intelligence for Computer Scientists is 12 units. Admission requires current undergraduate
status, a C or better in CSC 413 or an equivalent, and at least a 3.0 GPA; some electives
have extra prerequisites. The Graduate Certificate in Ethical Artificial Intelligence is a
10-unit interdisciplinary program covering AI technology, business ethics and compliance,
and philosophical ethics, with a research and reflection component. The Minor in
Artificial Intelligence for Business Applications is at least 12 units.

Source: SFSU Bulletin, bulletin.sfsu.edu (GenAI certificate, Ethical AI certificate, and AI for Business Applications minor pages; undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## College of Business AI Hub

According to SFSU's College of Business, the Business AI Hub is a separate applied-business
AI initiative that soft-launched in April 2026. It uses locally run open models in an
air-gapped environment. The AI Hub's location and hours are described as forthcoming. The
Business AI Hub is not part of the Student AI Commons.

Source: AI Hub | Lam-Larsen Initiatives and Centers, cob.sfsu.edu/initiatives-centers/ai-hub (undated, describes April 2026 soft launch, checked 2026-10-29).

## AI For All

According to SFSU News, AI For All is a planned Bay Area AI-literacy initiative involving
the Bay Area Council and SFSU. As of October 2026 it was an announced collaboration, not a
program with open registration.

Source: SFSU hosts inaugural AI hackathon, news.sfsu.edu/news/sfsu-hosts-inaugural-ai-hackathon (source date 2026-10-08, checked 2026-10-29).

## SFSU Library

The J. Paul Leonard Library is at 1630 Holloway Avenue. It offers research help by chat,
email, or appointment. General help: ask@sfsu.libanswers.com or 415-338-1552. Library
hours vary by day and service; check the Library's hours calendar at sfsu.libcal.com.

Source: J. Paul Leonard Library, library.sfsu.edu; LibCal, sfsu.libcal.com (both undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## SFSU Undergraduate Advising Center

SFSU's Undergraduate Advising Center is in ADM 203. Contact: uacadvising@sfsu.edu or
415-338-2101. Office and phone hours are Monday through Friday, 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM. The
Advising Center helps with degree planning, class selection, exploring majors, and policy
questions. Online appointments are linked from advising.sfsu.edu.

Source: Undergraduate Advising Center, advising.sfsu.edu (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## SFSU Career and Leadership Development

SFSU's Career and Leadership Development office is in ADM 254. Contact: career@sfsu.edu
or 415-338-1764. Hours are Monday through Friday, 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM, with drop-ins from
10 to 11 AM. It offers resume and interview help, career events, Handshake jobs and
internships, and Hiration career tools.

Source: Career and Leadership Development, career.sfsu.edu (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## SFSU tutoring (TASC)

SFSU's Tutoring and Academic Support Center (TASC) offers free peer tutoring in LIB 220 and
online. Contact: tutoring@sfsu.edu or 415-405-5516. TASC's Fall 2026 service runs August 24
to December 16. In-person hours are Monday through Thursday, 9 AM to 5 PM, and Friday,
10 AM to 2 PM. Students can book a one-time appointment or request weekly support on
tutoring.sfsu.edu.

Source: Tutoring & Academic Support, tutoring.sfsu.edu/tutoring (undated; schedule explicitly Fall 2026; checked 2026-10-29).

## SFSU IT help (ITS Service Desk)

SFSU's ITS Service Desk handles passwords, Wi-Fi, and account issues. It is in ADM 125.
Contact: service@sfsu.edu or 415-338-1420. Hours are Monday through Friday, 8 AM to 5 PM,
with Zoom support from 9 AM to 4 PM. Ticket and Zoom links are at its.sfsu.edu/support.

## SFSU disability services (DPRC)

SFSU's Disability Programs and Resource Center (DPRC) helps with accommodations and
disability-related support. It is in Student Services Building 110. Contact:
dprc@sfsu.edu, 415-338-2472, or video phone 415-335-7210. Hours are Monday through Friday,
8:30 AM to 5 PM. Registration and myDPRC are linked from access.sfsu.edu/student.

## SFSU counseling (CAPS)

SFSU's Counseling & Psychological Services (CAPS) offers counseling, same-day support,
drop-ins, groups, and workshops. CAPS is on the third floor of the Gator Student Health
Center, 730 Font Boulevard. Contact: caps@sfsu.edu or 415-338-2208. Hours are Monday
through Friday, 8 AM to 4:45 PM.

Source: Counseling & Psychological Services, caps.sfsu.edu (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## SFSU Student Health Services

SFSU's Student Health Services provides confidential basic health services, primary care,
pharmacy, and lab services. It is on the second floor of the Gator Student Health Center,
730 Font Boulevard. Contact: StudentHealthServices@sfsu.edu or 415-338-1251. Hours are
Monday through Thursday, 8:15 AM to 4:45 PM, and Friday, 9:15 AM to 4:45 PM. For medical
emergencies, Student Health Services directs people to call 911.

Source: Student Health Services, health.sfsu.edu (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Campus emergency numbers

SFSU's University Police says: in a campus emergency, dial 911 from a campus phone, or

Source: Crime Prevention | University Police Department, upd.sfsu.edu/crime-prevention (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## University Police non-emergency line

SFSU's University Police non-emergency and general information line is 415-338-7200,
staffed 24 hours a day. The upd@sfsu.edu email is not monitored 24/7 and is not for
immediate help.

Source: Contact Us | University Police Department, upd.sfsu.edu/contact-us (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Campus emergency phones

SFSU's marked emergency phones connect directly to University Police Dispatch. They are in
places including the Library and every level of the main parking garage. Campus maps mark
them with an "E".

Source: Campus Emergency Phones, upd.sfsu.edu/campus-emergency-phones (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## SFSU Alert emergency notifications

SFSU Alert sends emergency information and instructions. Students can add or update a
"Mobile Text" phone number in Student Center; employees use HR Self-Service. Emergency
email notification is mandatory for SFSU email holders.

Source: Emergency Notification System, oes.sfsu.edu/get-prepared/emergency-notification-system (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

November 11 for Veterans Day. Fall recess is November 23 to 28: campus is open November 23

## Getting to SFSU by Muni

The Muni M Ocean View train stops at 19th Avenue & Holloway Avenue (SF State). It connects
campus with West Portal and the Market Street subway toward downtown, and with Balboa Park
in the other direction. Muni buses 28 and 28R connect Daly City BART with campus, and the
57 serves the west side of campus. Check SFMTA (sfmta.com) for live arrivals and service
changes.

Source: M Ocean View | SFMTA, sfmta.com/routes/m-ocean-view; MUNI | Parking & Transportation, parking.sfsu.edu/muni (both undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Student transit pass (Gator Pass)

According to SFSU Sustainability, the Gator Pass (Clipper BayPass) gives students transit
access during the fall and spring semesters on Muni, BART, Caltrain, and other
participating systems. It is a student benefit, not free transit for visitors.

Source: How We Commute, sustain.sfsu.edu/how-we-commute (page undated; subsection updated January 2025; checked 2026-10-29).

## Visitor parking at SFSU

According to SFSU Parking & Transportation, visitors and the general public can park in
Lot 20 on State Drive and Lot 25 on Winston Drive. A valid permit is required at all
times, and daily parking is paid with PayByPhone. Parking office: parking@sfsu.edu or
415-338-1441.

Source: PayByPhone at SFSU, parking.sfsu.edu/paybyphone-sfsu (undated; payment change effective 2025-06-01; checked 2026-10-29).
