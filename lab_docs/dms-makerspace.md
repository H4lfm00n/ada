## What the Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace is

The Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace, often called DMS, is listed on the website of
SFSU's Academic Technology office. It serves SFSU students, faculty, and staff working on digital media
and making projects. The AI Studio, which serves the Student AI Commons, is inside it.

Source: Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace, at.sfsu.edu/digital-media-studio-and-makerspace (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Where the Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace is

The Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace is in room LIB 260, on the second floor. Its desk
phone is 415-338-1490, its email is at@sfsu.edu, and its Instagram is
@sfsu.dms.makerspace.

Source: Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace, at.sfsu.edu/digital-media-studio-and-makerspace (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace hours

According to its October 28, 2026 notice, the Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace is open
during the semester Monday through Thursday, 10 AM to 7 PM, and Friday, 10 AM to 5 PM. It
offers drop-in exploration during those hours.

Holiday closures are not listed there; check the SFSU academic calendar.

Source: Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace: November events, at.sfsu.edu/news/digital-media-studio-and-makerspace-november-events (source date 2026-10-28, checked 2026-10-29).

## Rooms in the Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace

The Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace has rooms for audio production and mixing,
fabrication, green screen and multi-use work, podcasting, sewing, and VR development. The
podcast setup has a RØDECaster Pro II, four PodMic microphones, headphones, and an iMac.
The VR development room has HTC Vive and Oculus equipment for building games,
simulations, and interactive experiences.

Its rooms page also lists the Edge Lab of the Lam Family College of Business, which is in
room BUS 205C, not in LIB 260.

Source: Rooms and Spaces, at.sfsu.edu/digital-media-studio-makerspace/rooms-spaces (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Equipment at the Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace

The Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace has high-end computers with Adobe Creative Suite,
Final Cut Pro, and Pro Tools, plus scanners, printing, 3D printers (models UM3 and
UMS5), a staff-operated Epilog laser cutter, a Cricut Explore 4, and VR headsets.

It lends cameras (including a Canon EOS RP, a GoPro HERO 11, and 360 cameras), RØDE
microphones, recorders, lights, tripods, and electronics prototyping kits (Arduino Mega
2560 and Uno, Raspberry Pi, micro:bit, and LittleBits).

Sources: Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace, at.sfsu.edu/digital-media-studio-and-makerspace; Equipment Checkout, at.sfsu.edu/digital-media-studio-makerspace/equipment-checkout; Services, at.sfsu.edu/digital-media-studio-makerspace/services (all undated, checked 2026-10-29).
