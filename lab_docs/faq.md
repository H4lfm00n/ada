## Where is the AI Studio?

The AI Studio is in room LIB 260, on the second floor, inside the Digital Media Studio and
MakerSpace. It serves the SFSU Student AI Commons.

Source: Academic Technology Updates: Fall 2026, at.sfsu.edu/news/academic-technology-updates-fall-2026 (source date 2026-08-24, checked 2026-10-29).

## Is the AI Studio the same as the Digital Media Studio?

No. The AI Studio is a space inside the Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace, in LIB 260,
that serves the Student AI Commons.

Source: AI Studio Grand Opening, ai.sfsu.edu/event/ai-studio-grand-opening (event 2026-09-29, checked 2026-10-29).

## Can I just stop by the AI Studio?

Yes, during the Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace's posted hours, which offer drop-in
exploration. Individual equipment and rooms have extra rules, so ask at the front desk
before using them.

Source: Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace: November events, at.sfsu.edu/news/digital-media-studio-and-makerspace-november-events (source date 2026-10-28, checked 2026-10-29).

## How do I hear about AI Commons events?

Check the Student AI Commons programming page at at.sfsu.edu/event/student-ai-commons,
and follow @sfsuaistudentcommons on Instagram.

Sources: SFSU Student AI Commons Events, at.sfsu.edu/event/student-ai-commons (undated, checked 2026-10-29); AI for Students, ai.sfsu.edu/ai-students (undated, checked 2026-10-29).
