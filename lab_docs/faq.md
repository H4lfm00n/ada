## Where is the AI Studio?

The AI Studio is in room LIB 260, on the second floor, inside the Digital Media Studio and
MakerSpace. The AI Studio serves the SFSU Student AI Commons.

Source: Academic Technology Updates: Fall 2026, at.sfsu.edu/news/academic-technology-updates-fall-2026 (source date 2026-08-24, checked 2026-10-29).

## Is the AI Studio the same as the Digital Media Studio?

No. The AI Studio is a space inside the Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace in LIB 260, and
it serves the Student AI Commons.

Source: AI Studio Grand Opening, ai.sfsu.edu/event/ai-studio-grand-opening (event 2026-09-29, checked 2026-10-29).

## How do I join the Student AI Commons?

Use the "Join now!" link on SFSU's AI for Students page, ai.sfsu.edu/ai-students. The same
page links the Student AI Commons Discord and Instagram.

Source: AI for Students, ai.sfsu.edu/ai-students (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Do I need to join a club to visit the AI Studio?

No membership rule for visiting the AI Studio has been published. The Digital Media Studio
and MakerSpace offers drop-in exploration during its posted hours. Ask the staff before
using specific equipment or rooms.

Source: AI for Students, ai.sfsu.edu/ai-students (undated, checked 2026-10-29); Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace: November events, at.sfsu.edu/news/digital-media-studio-and-makerspace-november-events (source date 2026-10-28, checked 2026-10-29).

## How do I hear about Student AI Commons events?

Check the Commons programming page, at.sfsu.edu/event/student-ai-commons, and the student
AI club listings it links. The Student AI Commons Discord (discord.gg/XSmfEh25) and
Instagram (@sfsuaistudentcommons) are linked from ai.sfsu.edu/ai-students.

Source: SFSU Student AI Commons | Events, at.sfsu.edu/event/student-ai-commons (undated, checked 2026-10-29); AI for Students, ai.sfsu.edu/ai-students (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Can I train a model overnight or connect remotely?

No public policy for GPU booking, remote access, or overnight compute has been published
for the Student AI Commons. Ask the LIB 260 desk staff or email at@sfsu.edu.

Source: SFSU Student AI Commons | Events, at.sfsu.edu/event/student-ai-commons (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Where can I see examples of student AI work?

SFSU's hackathon winners recap describes three projects: GatorPlate, Plan-Ed, and
Throughline. The SFSU News article on the first Student AI Awards links to the awards
showcase.

Source: Meet the winning teams, ai.sfsu.edu/news/meet-winning-teams-2026-sf-hacks-x-gdg-ai-hackathon (source date 2026-10-05, checked 2026-10-29); SFSU announces winners of its first-ever student AI awards, news.sfsu.edu/news/sfsu-announces-winners-its-first-ever-student-ai-awards-0 (source date 2026-05-18, checked 2026-10-29).

## Is the Student AI Commons the same as the CSU AI Commons?

No. The CSU AI Commons is a set of systemwide California State University AI resources.
The SFSU Student AI Commons is SFSU's student AI community, served by the AI Studio in
LIB 260.

Source: AI for Students, ai.sfsu.edu/ai-students (undated, checked 2026-10-29).
