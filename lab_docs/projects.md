## Student AI Commons projects

The SFSU Student AI Commons supports student-proposed projects. Students submit a project
proposal through the student request form, and the AI Commons team follows up to discuss
the software and resources the project needs. No named Commons projects have been
published yet.

Source: AI Lab PM (Atharva), confirmed 2026-11-10.

## SF Hacks x GDG AI Hackathon winning projects

The SF Hacks x GDG AI Hackathon on October 3, 2026, sponsored by the Student AI Commons,
named three SFSU-track winners. GatorPlate helps people find and post leftover food. Plan-Ed
is AI-assisted degree planning with a rules engine. Throughline identifies the course
knowledge a class assumes and helps plan review. These are hackathon projects, not running
SFSU services.

Source: Meet the winning teams from the 2026 SF Hacks X GDG AI Hackathon, ai.sfsu.edu/news/meet-winning-teams-2026-sf-hacks-x-gdg-ai-hackathon (source date 2026-10-05, checked 2026-10-29).

## SFSU Student AI Awards projects

SFSU's first Student AI Awards, presented May 5, 2026, recognized student projects
including Shipyard, a hackathon-management tool, and BlueCore AI, which supports maritime
workers. The SFSU News article about the awards links to the awards showcase.

Source: SFSU announces winners of its first-ever student AI awards, news.sfsu.edu/news/sfsu-announces-winners-its-first-ever-student-ai-awards-0 (source date 2026-05-18, checked 2026-10-29).

## Student AI projects at the MakerSpace Open House

The Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace Open House on November 5, 2026 included
presentations of student AI and edge-computing projects. The project names were not
published.

Source: Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace Open House, at.sfsu.edu/event/dms-makerspace-open-house-nov-5-2026 (event 2026-11-05, checked 2026-10-29).
