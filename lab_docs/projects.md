## Student AI projects from the fall 2026 AI hackathon

At SFSU's first AI hackathon in October 2026, the SFSU-track winning projects were:
GatorPlate, which helps students find and post leftover food; Plan-Ed, which uses AI and
a rules engine to help with degree planning; and Throughline, which identifies the
course knowledge a class assumes and helps plan review. These are hackathon projects,
not running campus services or AI Lab projects.

Source: Meet the winning teams from the 2026 SF Hacks X GDG AI Hackathon, ai.sfsu.edu/news/meet-winning-teams-2026-sf-hacks-x-gdg-ai-hackathon (source date 2026-10-05, checked 2026-10-29).

## Student AI projects from the 2026 Student AI Awards

Projects recognized at SFSU's first Student AI Awards in May 2026 include Shipyard, a
hackathon management tool, and BlueCore AI, which supports maritime workers. The SFSU
News article links the full awards showcase.

Source: SFSU announces winners of its first-ever student AI awards, news.sfsu.edu/news/sfsu-announces-winners-its-first-ever-student-ai-awards-0 (source date 2026-05-18, checked 2026-10-29).
