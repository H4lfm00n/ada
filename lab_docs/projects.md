## SF Hacks x GDG AI Hackathon winning projects

The October 2026 SF Hacks x GDG AI Hackathon, sponsored by the Student AI Commons, named
three SFSU-track winners. GatorPlate helps people find and post leftover food. Plan-Ed is
AI-assisted degree planning with a rules engine. Throughline identifies the course
knowledge a class assumes and helps plan review. These are hackathon projects, not running
SFSU services.

Source: Meet the winning teams from the 2026 SF Hacks X GDG AI Hackathon, ai.sfsu.edu/news/meet-winning-teams-2026-sf-hacks-x-gdg-ai-hackathon (source date 2026-10-05, checked 2026-10-29).

## SFSU Student AI Awards projects

SFSU's first Student AI Awards, presented May 5, 2026, recognized student projects
including Shipyard, a hackathon-management tool, and BlueCore AI, which supports maritime
workers. The SFSU News article about the awards links to the awards showcase.

Source: SFSU announces winners of its first-ever student AI awards, news.sfsu.edu/news/sfsu-announces-winners-its-first-ever-student-ai-awards-0 (source date 2026-05-18, checked 2026-10-29).
