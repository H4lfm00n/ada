## What Academic Technology is

Academic Technology (AT) is SFSU's central IT unit within Academic Affairs. It supports
faculty, staff, and students wherever teaching uses technology. AT's stated purpose is to
support teaching, learning, and academic excellence. AT works in four areas: classroom
technology, learning technology, academic IT support, and academic IT leadership. AT
partners with ITS on campus IT projects.

Source: How to Get Started, facaffairs.sfsu.edu/how-get-started; What We Do, at.sfsu.edu/what-we-do (both undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Academic Technology services

Academic Technology's services include classroom audio-visual design and support, Canvas,
Zoom, the Syllabus tool, teaching and learning innovation, access to research and teaching
software, equipment loans for faculty and staff, and desktop and website support for
participating Academic Affairs units. The Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace is
documented on AT's website, and AT announces Student AI Commons programming.

Source: Services, at.sfsu.edu/services (undated, checked 2026-10-29); Academic Technology Updates: Fall 2026, at.sfsu.edu/news/academic-technology-updates-fall-2026 (source date 2026-08-24, checked 2026-10-29).

## How to contact Academic Technology

Academic Technology Services is in LIB 80. Contact: at@sfsu.edu or 415-405-5555. AT's
support page, at.sfsu.edu/get-help, links appointments, AT Help articles, tutorials,
service status, and technology request forms.

Source: Get Help | Academic Technology, at.sfsu.edu/get-help (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Borrowing a laptop from Academic Technology

Academic Technology lends laptops for free from LIB 80 to currently enrolled SFSU students,
for short-term or semester loans, subject to availability. Bring your SFSU ID. Borrowers
are financially responsible for loss, damage, or late return. Semester loans are
requested with a form on at.sfsu.edu/laptop-checkout; requests are reviewed and supply is
limited.

Source: Laptop Checkout | Academic Technology, at.sfsu.edu/laptop-checkout (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Adobe Creative Cloud for SFSU students

According to SFSU ITS, all SFSU faculty, staff, and students can install Adobe Creative
Cloud with their SFSU account, signed in on at most two computers. Acrobat AI Assistant is
listed for faculty and staff.

Source: Adobe Tools Creative Cloud, its.sfsu.edu/service/adobecreativecloudcc (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Campus AI tools at SFSU

SFSU's AI Tools page, ai.sfsu.edu/tools, lists ChatGPT Edu, Microsoft Copilot, Adobe AI
features, Box AI, Khanmigo in Canvas, Poll Everywhere AI, Qualtrics AI features, and Zoom AI
Companion. Eligibility varies by tool, and Khanmigo is for faculty only. A tool being
listed doesn't mean every feature is turned on for every account; check with the support
office named for that tool.

Source: Tools | AI, ai.sfsu.edu/tools (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## ChatGPT Edu at SFSU

According to SFSU's ChatGPT Edu page, ChatGPT Edu comes from a CSU–OpenAI enterprise
agreement. Users sign in with their SFSU email address and then campus authentication. The
page says ChatGPT Edu conversations and data are not used to train OpenAI's models.

Source: OpenAI ChatGPT Edu, ai.sfsu.edu/chatgpt-edu (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Student AI Literacy Essentials course

Academic Technology developed Student AI Literacy Essentials, a free, self-paced Canvas
course offered across the CSU through the CSU Chancellor's Office. A 100% course grade
earns a badge. According to its page, testing took about 3.5 to 4 hours.

Source: Student AI Literacy Essentials, ai.sfsu.edu/student-ai-literacy-essentials (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## AI Literacy Education Program

The AI Literacy Education Program is a collaboration among Academic Technology, CEETL, and
ITS. Students and community members may attend its courses, but its digital badge is for
current SFSU faculty and staff. Students who want a badge are directed to the Student AI
Literacy Essentials course instead.

Source: Start your AI journey, ai.sfsu.edu/start-your-ai-journey (undated; Fall 2026 course schedule; checked 2026-10-29).

## Is Academic Technology the same as the ITS Service Desk?

No. Academic Technology is part of Academic Affairs and focuses on teaching and learning
technology, like Canvas and Zoom. The ITS Service Desk handles accounts, passwords, and
Wi-Fi.

Source: How to Get Started, facaffairs.sfsu.edu/how-get-started; Get Help | ITS, its.sfsu.edu/support (both undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Can a student earn an AI literacy badge?

Yes. SFSU students can earn a badge by getting 100% in the self-paced Student AI Literacy
Essentials course. That badge is separate from the faculty and staff AI Literacy Education
Program badge.

Source: Student AI Literacy Essentials, ai.sfsu.edu/student-ai-literacy-essentials; Start your AI journey, ai.sfsu.edu/start-your-ai-journey (both undated, checked 2026-10-29).
