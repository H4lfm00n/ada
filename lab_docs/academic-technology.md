## What Academic Technology does

Academic Technology (AT) is SFSU's central technology unit within Academic Affairs. It
supports teaching and learning with technology, including classroom audio and video,
Canvas, Zoom, the Syllabus tool, and research and teaching software. AT works with
Information Technology Services (ITS) on campus IT projects. AT announces the Student AI
Commons programming, and its website lists the Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace.

Sources: What We Do, at.sfsu.edu/what-we-do; Services, at.sfsu.edu/services; How to Get Started, facaffairs.sfsu.edu/how-get-started (all undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Academic Technology versus the ITS Service Desk

Academic Technology focuses on teaching and learning technology, like Canvas and Zoom.
The ITS Service Desk handles accounts, passwords, and Wi-Fi.

Sources: How to Get Started, facaffairs.sfsu.edu/how-get-started; Get Help, its.sfsu.edu/support (both undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Canvas and Zoom help from Academic Technology

For Canvas or Zoom help, contact Academic Technology in LIB 80, at at@sfsu.edu or
415-405-5555. Its support page, at.sfsu.edu/get-help, links help articles, tutorials,
service status, and appointment booking.

Source: Get Help, Academic Technology, at.sfsu.edu/get-help (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Borrowing a laptop from Academic Technology

Currently enrolled SFSU students can borrow a laptop for free from Academic Technology in
LIB 80, subject to availability. Bring your SFSU ID. Borrowers are responsible for loss,
damage, or late return. For a semester-long loan, use the request form on AT's Laptop
Checkout page; requests are reviewed and supply is limited.

## Free software for SFSU students

All SFSU students, faculty, and staff can install Adobe Creative Cloud with their SFSU
account, on up to two computers at a time. Matriculated students are eligible for
Microsoft Office 365.

Sources: Adobe Creative Cloud, its.sfsu.edu/service/adobecreativecloudcc; Technology Resources for Students, at.sfsu.edu/resources-student (both undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## AI tools offered at SFSU

SFSU's AI Tools page lists ChatGPT Edu, Microsoft Copilot, Adobe AI features, Box AI,
Khanmigo in Canvas (faculty only), Poll Everywhere AI, Qualtrics AI features, and Zoom
AI Companion. Who can use each tool varies, and being listed doesn't mean every feature is
turned on for every account. Check with the support office for a specific tool.

Source: Tools, ai.sfsu.edu/tools (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Free AI literacy courses at SFSU

Student AI Literacy Essentials is a free, self-paced Canvas course built by SFSU's
Academic Technology and offered across the CSU. Students who score 100% earn a digital
badge, and testing found it takes about 3.5 to 4 hours.

The separate AI Literacy Education Program, run by Academic Technology, CEETL, and ITS,
offers live courses, such as its free November 2026 sessions on Zoom. Students and
community members may attend, but that program's badge is for SFSU faculty and staff.
