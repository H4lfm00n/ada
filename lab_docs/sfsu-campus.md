## About San Francisco State University

San Francisco State University (SFSU, also called SF State) is a public urban university
in San Francisco and part of the California State University (CSU) system. According to
its About page, its mission emphasizes academic excellence, creativity, a diverse
community, social justice, and positive change.

Source: About San Francisco State University, sfsu.edu/about (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## SFSU address and main phone number

SFSU's main campus is at 1600 Holloway Avenue, San Francisco, CA 94132, in the southwest
part of the city. The main campus covers 144 acres. The university's general information
number is 415-338-1111. A campus map is at maps.sfsu.edu.

Sources: About San Francisco State University, sfsu.edu/about (undated, checked 2026-10-29); Campus Maps, maps.sfsu.edu (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## SFSU's colleges

SFSU's Academics page lists these colleges: the Lam Family College of Business; Ethnic
Studies; Health and Social Sciences; Liberal and Creative Arts; Professional and Global
Education; Science and Engineering; and the Graduate College of Education.

Source: Academics, sfsu.edu/academics (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Getting to SFSU by Muni Metro

The Muni M Ocean View line stops at 19th Avenue and Holloway Avenue (SF State). It
connects campus with West Portal and the Market Street subway toward downtown in one
direction, and Balboa Park in the other. Check SFMTA's M Ocean View page for live
arrivals and service changes.

Source: M Ocean View, sfmta.com/routes/m-ocean-view (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Getting to SFSU by bus and BART

Muni buses 28 and 28R connect Daly City BART station with campus. The Muni 57 serves the
west side of campus.

The SF State shuttle from Daly City BART was discontinued in Fall 2024, according to
SFSU's Office of Sustainability. Use Muni instead.

Sources: MUNI, parking.sfsu.edu/muni (undated, checked 2026-10-29); How We Commute, sustain.sfsu.edu/how-we-commute (undated page, checked 2026-10-29); CampusMemo, campusmemo.sfsu.edu/2024/july-15-2024 (source date 2024-07-15, checked 2026-10-29).

## Student transit passes

SFSU's Office of Sustainability describes the Gator Pass (Clipper BayPass) as transit
access for students during the fall and spring semesters, covering Muni, BART, Caltrain,
and other participating systems. It is a student benefit, not free transit for visitors.

## Visitor parking at SFSU

SFSU Parking lists Lot 20 on State Drive and Lot 25 on Winston Drive for visitors and the
general public. A valid permit is required at all times, and daily parking is paid with
the PayByPhone app. The Parking office is at parking@sfsu.edu and 415-338-1441.

Source: PayByPhone at SFSU, parking.sfsu.edu/paybyphone-sfsu (undated, checked 2026-10-29).
