## About these dates

These dates come from the SFSU Registrar's Dates and Deadlines page, last updated
October 13, 2026. The Registrar labels Fall 2026 and Spring 2027 dates as tentative and
subject to change. For official deadlines, check registrar.sfsu.edu/deadlines, or contact
the Registrar at registrar@sfsu.edu or 415-338-2350.

Source: Dates and Deadlines, registrar.sfsu.edu/deadlines (updated 2026-10-13, checked 2026-10-29).

## Fall 2026 semester dates

According to the Registrar, Fall 2026 instruction began August 24, 2026. Campus is closed
for Veterans Day on November 11. Fall recess is November 23 to 28: campus is open
November 23 to 25 and closed November 26 to 28. Instruction ends December 11, and final
exams are December 12 to 18.

Source: Dates and Deadlines, registrar.sfsu.edu/deadlines (updated 2026-10-13, checked 2026-10-29).

## Spring 2027 semester dates

According to the Registrar's tentative calendar, Spring 2027 instruction begins
January 25, 2027. Spring recess is March 22 to 27, with campus open. Campus is closed
March 31 for Farmworkers Day. Instruction ends May 14, and final exams are May 15 to 21.

Source: Dates and Deadlines, registrar.sfsu.edu/deadlines (updated 2026-10-13, checked 2026-10-29).
