## Borrowing cameras, audio, and A/V gear from the MakerSpace

According to the Digital Media Studio and MakerSpace's published checkout policy, A/V
equipment is first come, first served, with priority for students. Borrowers need a valid
SFSU OneCard and the required training. Loans last up to three business days, and
checkouts and returns stop one hour before closing. Late returns cost $50 per day, and
loss or damage can cost up to $2,000. Faculty and staff should ask Academic Technology
about other options.

Source: Equipment Checkout, at.sfsu.edu/digital-media-studio-makerspace/equipment-checkout (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Borrowing robotics and electronics kits from the MakerSpace

According to the MakerSpace's published policy, robotics and mechatronics kits (such as
Arduino and Raspberry Pi kits) need a valid SFSU OneCard. Loans last four hours and can be
extended with approval. Kits must come back in their original condition.

Source: Equipment Checkout, at.sfsu.edu/digital-media-studio-makerspace/equipment-checkout (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## 3D printing at the MakerSpace

According to the MakerSpace's published policy, staff approve every 3D model and do the
slicing. Models must be lawful and noncommercial, and weapons, violent, or inappropriate
models aren't allowed. Submit an STL file. Each print must finish within seven hours, and
overnight printing isn't allowed. PLA costs $0.084 per gram, with a $1 minimum, paid in
advance.

Source: Services, at.sfsu.edu/digital-media-studio-makerspace/services (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Laser cutting at the MakerSpace

According to the MakerSpace's published policy, only staff operate the laser cutter. You
need an appointment, and materials must be provided or approved by the MakerSpace.

Source: Services, at.sfsu.edu/digital-media-studio-makerspace/services (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Using the Cricut at the MakerSpace

The MakerSpace's Cricut Explore 4 is for decals, labels, stickers, and cardstock projects.
Users need basic knowledge of how to run it. A small amount of material is provided for
small projects.

Source: Services, at.sfsu.edu/digital-media-studio-makerspace/services (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## VR sessions at the MakerSpace

According to the MakerSpace's published policy, VR sessions need a valid SFSU ID,
check-in, a signed waiver, a spotter, and at least two people. Sessions last 15 minutes
and depend on staffing and scheduling.

Source: Services, at.sfsu.edu/digital-media-studio-makerspace/services (undated, checked 2026-10-29).

## Audio rooms and the sewing room at the MakerSpace

According to the MakerSpace's published rules, the audio rooms are for audio work only,
with no food or drink. Use is generally limited to two hours a day, with extensions when
the room is free. Rooms and equipment must never be left unattended.

The sewing room is walk-in, with sessions limited to two hours when it's busy. The Juki
sewing machine is only for current students who already know how to use it.

Source: Rooms and Spaces, at.sfsu.edu/digital-media-studio-makerspace/rooms-spaces (undated, checked 2026-10-29).
