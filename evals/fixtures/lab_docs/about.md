## What the AI Lab is

The AI Lab at SFSU is a student-founded lab where students build projects in artificial
intelligence and robotics. The AI Lab focuses on hands-on learning: members learn AI by
building real things together.

## Who the AI Lab is for

The AI Lab is open to all SFSU students, in any major and at any experience level.

## What AI Lab members do

AI Lab members work in small teams on hands-on projects. They share what they learn at
weekly general meetings and help newer members get started.

## The AI Lab and SFSU

The AI Lab is a student-run group at SFSU. It is not an official university office and
does not speak for SFSU.

## The lab's robot assistant

The AI Lab's robot assistant is an AI that runs entirely on a computer in the lab. It was
built by people in the AI Lab.
