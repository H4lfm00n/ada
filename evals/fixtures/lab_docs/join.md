## How to join the AI Lab

To join the AI Lab, come to any general meeting and fill out the member form that is
shared at the meeting. After attending two general meetings, you are an AI Lab member.

## Does joining the AI Lab cost money?

Joining the AI Lab is free. There are no membership fees.

## Experience needed to join the AI Lab

No AI or programming experience is needed to join the AI Lab. Beginners are welcome.

## Which majors can join the AI Lab

Students in any major can join the AI Lab.

## Your first visit to the AI Lab

At your first AI Lab meeting, say hi to an officer at the front table and they will
introduce you to the current projects. Bringing a laptop is helpful but not required.

## Visiting the AI Lab as faculty or a guest

Faculty and guests are welcome to visit AI Lab general meetings. No sign-up is needed.
