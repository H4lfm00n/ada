## Who can use AI Lab equipment

AI Lab equipment is for AI Lab members, after a short safety walkthrough with a lab
officer. AI Lab equipment stays in the lab.

## AI Lab computer: NVIDIA GB10

The AI Lab has an NVIDIA GB10 computer with 128 GB of memory shared by its CPU and GPU.
The lab's robot assistant runs on it. Approved AI Lab projects can also use it.

## AI Lab equipment: 3D printer

The AI Lab has a 3D printer for project parts. The 3D printer is available Monday
through Thursday.

## AI Lab equipment safety

Ask a lab officer before using any AI Lab equipment for the first time.
