## AI Lab general meetings

AI Lab general meetings are held every Wednesday at 5:30 PM in Room E-217, Example Hall,
during the fall and spring semesters.

No AI Lab general meetings are held during finals week.

## AI Lab work sessions

AI Lab work sessions are held every Friday from 3 PM to 6 PM in Room E-217, Example Hall.
Work sessions are open to AI Lab members.

## Recurring AI Lab events

Build Night is an AI Lab event held on the first Friday of each month. At Build Night,
members demo what they built and newcomers can try the lab's projects. Everyone is
welcome at Build Night.
