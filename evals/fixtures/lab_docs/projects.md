## How to get involved in an AI Lab project

To join an AI Lab project, ask about it at a general meeting. New members can join any
project, and no experience is required.

## AI Lab project: the lab's robot assistant

The lab's robot assistant is the AI you can chat with on the screen in the lab. It runs
entirely on a computer in the lab, with no cloud services. Status: in active development.

## AI Lab project: robot arm

The AI Lab is building a robot arm from an SO-ARM101 kit. The goal is to teach the arm
to pick up and sort small objects. Status: planned.

## AI Lab project: Campus Plant Doctor

Campus Plant Doctor is an AI Lab project that uses an image classifier to spot diseases
on plant leaves from a phone photo. Status: in progress.
